**Last Updated:** 2026-08-26

## Purpose

This document is a parking area for ideas, proposals, experiments, and possible future improvements that are **not approved project requirements**.

It is **not** a formal specification and it is **not** a development schedule.

Formal requirements belong in [MASTER_SPECIFICATION.md](./MASTER_SPECIFICATION.md).

Scheduled work belongs in [ROADMAP.md](./ROADMAP.md).

Design rationale belongs in [docs/design_history.md](./docs/design_history.md).

---

## Status Definitions

- **Idea** — an unassessed possibility.
- **Proposal** — an idea worth evaluating.
- **Experimental** — being tested but not approved as a requirement.
- **Accepted for Roadmap** — approved to be scheduled in `ROADMAP.md`, but not automatically a specification change.
- **Rejected** — explicitly rejected.
- **Superseded** — replaced by another idea or approach.

## Rules

- Ideas are not specifications.
- Ideas are not automatically roadmap commitments.
- Do not move an idea into `MASTER_SPECIFICATION.md` without explicit approval.
- Do not treat an idea as an implemented feature.
- Do not invent historical ideas or decisions.
- When an idea becomes an approved development task, record the task in `ROADMAP.md`.
- When an idea changes a formal requirement, obtain explicit project-owner approval and record the approved specification change in `CHANGELOG.md`.
